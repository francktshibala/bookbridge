# Social Media Strategy Update - Hybrid Approach

**Date:** December 2024  
**Status:** Recommended Implementation

---

## 🎯 End Result Goal

- Grow ESL app users via social media
- Convert viewers to app users
- Monetize later (freemium model)

---

## ❌ Current Problem

- Low views/engagement (new channel, normal but slow)
- AI-only narration limits human connection
- Off-platform CTAs underperform (platforms punish external links)
- Emotional stories are niche (not mass appeal)
- Serialized format requires commitment (harder to start)

---

## ✅ Recommended Hybrid Approach

### Keep What Works
- ✅ Serialized story format (proven engagement from TikTok data)
- ✅ Story-first approach (emotional stories outperform lessons)
- ✅ 0.70x speed consistency ("Slow English" branding)
- ✅ Multi-part habit-building (10-minute parts)

### Add GPT's Insights
- ✅ **On-camera intro** (30 seconds): "Same story, three levels"
- ✅ **Level switching demo** within same story part (A1 → B1 → C1 preview)
- ✅ **Engagement hook**: "Which level felt comfortable? Comment below"

---

## 📋 Implementation Structure

**Video Format (10 minutes):**
1. **On-camera intro** (30s): "Same story, three levels of English"
2. **Hook** (30s): Emotional story opening
3. **Level switching demo** (60s): Show A1 → B1 → C1 of same scene
4. **Main story** (6 min): Full Part 1 at chosen level (A1/B1/C1)
5. **Vocabulary** (90s): Key words
6. **Shadowing** (2 min): Practice sentences
7. **Cliffhanger** (30s): Part 2 tease
8. **CTA** (15s): "Which level fit you? Comment A1, B1, or C1"

---

## 🎯 Why This Works

- **Maintains story-first** (your proven data)
- **Adds human connection** (GPT's key insight)
- **Showcases multi-level value** (app's core feature)
- **Keeps serialization** (habit-building strength)
- **Improves engagement** (comment hook drives retention)
